# Economic agency tiers and embedded-finance reservation

The July 22 brainstorm adds a product progression to Victoroff OS. It does not change constitutional
authority and does not authorize a financial product.

## Tier progression

Victoroff progresses through five identities: Foundation, Earner, Builder, Operator, and Business
Owner. The arc is increasing economic agency: control yourself, income, capital, systems, then the
institution. Tier V may reveal institutional operating capabilities, but it does not create a legal
entity, confer governance authority, or promise financial success.

Progress must come from versioned criteria and sourced, fresh inputs. Example percentages in the
brainstorm are illustrative only. If inputs are missing, Victoroff shows unknown; it never turns
missing data into zero or invents completion. Every state explains the calculation and returns one
valid next action.

The binding contract is
[`economic-agency-tiers.v1.json`](../../program/product/economic-agency-tiers.v1.json).

## Embedded finance

Cards and accounts are reserved as a future core layer so the OS does not make architecture choices
that foreclose them. The reservation is deliberately provider-neutral: Victoroff would own the
experience and governance while appropriately authorized providers own regulated account, issuing,
and network roles.

This repository does not authorize a card, account, payment, credit, money movement, or banking
implementation. A bounded product decision, qualified legal/regulatory review, compliance owner,
partner diligence, privacy/security/fraud/dispute controls, precise claims review, pilot rollback,
and vendor-exit plan are all required first. Vendor examples and insurance language from the
brainstorm are not adopted as current facts or commitments.

The binding reservation is
[`embedded-finance-reservation.v1.json`](../../program/product/embedded-finance-reservation.v1.json).

The separate Victoroff Commercial repository, official deck, business plan, and AI-assisted
before/after evidence remain authority-held under the existing three-program contract. This new
source reaffirms that lane; it does not create the repository or move commercial material into the
product repository.
