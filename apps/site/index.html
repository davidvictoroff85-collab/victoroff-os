01

Hold

Supplier bank details changed

Verified rule

A payment to changed bank details stays on hold until the change is independently verified and a Finance Controller records a release decision.

Timing Hold until verification and the Controller decision are both on record.

Prepare

  • Locate the change request and the channel it arrived through.
  • Record the independent call-back or verification result.
  • Route the release decision to the named Finance Controller.

02

Exception

Invoice and payment exception

Verified rule

An invoice match does not authorize release. Mismatched amount, payee, or terms requires a documented exception decision by the exception owner.

Timing Resolve before the scheduled payment run, or hold the item.

Prepare

  • Name the specific mismatch: amount, payee, terms, or reference.
  • Attach the invoice, purchase order, and receiving record.
  • Record who decided the exception and under what policy.

03

Delegation

Agent action outside its delegation

Verified rule

A connected tool is not permission. An agent may only act within its recorded delegation; anything outside it requires a human decision.

Timing Stop the action until a person with authority decides.

Prepare

  • Identify the agent, the requested action, and the tool it would use.
  • Compare the action with the agent's recorded delegation and limits.
  • Record the human decision and the authority it rests on.

04

Threshold

Above-threshold payment approval

Verified rule

Payments above the threshold require two approvers with recorded authority for the amount; the initiator cannot be one of them.

Timing Release only after both approvals are on record.

Prepare

  • Confirm the amount against the current threshold.
  • Check that each approver's authority covers this amount and category.
  • Confirm the initiator is not approving their own request.

05

Cross-party

Bank, fintech, and provider handoff

Verified rule

Each party owns part of the record. A handoff is explained only when the owner and the evidence for each step are identified.

Timing Agree record ownership before reconstructing the outcome.

Prepare

  • List each party and the step it performed.
  • Identify which records each party holds.
  • Mark every step where ownership or evidence is not established.

Evidence

Missing evidence or unexplained outcome

Verified rule

Supported findings and missing evidence are reported separately. A gap is recorded as a gap, never filled with assumption.

Timing Record the gap before any conclusion is shared.

Prepare

  • State the outcome and the question it raises.
  • Collect the records that exist and note their sources.
  • List what is missing and who would hold it.
